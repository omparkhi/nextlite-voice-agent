## 3. Appointment Booking Rules & Guidelines

### Advance Booking Window
- Appointments can be booked up to **7 days in advance**.
- Appointments cannot be scheduled more than 7 days ahead of the current date.
- All appointments are subject to the doctor's rostered schedule and available time slots.

### Walk-in Policy
- **Walk-in patients are welcome** during regular OPD hours across all 6 departments.
- **Priority Notice:** Pre-booked appointments receive priority. Walk-in patients will be seen based on token numbers and doctor availability, and may experience waiting times during busy periods.

### Arrival Time
- Patients with a confirmed appointment are requested to arrive **approximately 10 minutes prior** to their scheduled time to complete digital check-in and vitals recording.

### Required Patient Information for Booking
Before scheduling an appointment, the receptionist must gather:
1. Patient's Full Name
2. Contact Mobile Phone Number
3. Department or Preferred Doctor Name
4. Preferred Date
5. Preferred Time Slot

## 4. Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

### Cancellation Terms
- Patients should cancel their appointment at least **4 hours in advance** by calling the appointment desk at +91 70000 12346.
- Cancellations made less than 4 hours before the appointment or non-attendance will be treated as a missed appointment.
- Cancellation does not guarantee a refund if payment has already been processed at the counter or online.

### Rescheduling Terms
- Appointments may be rescheduled subject to doctor availability and available slot capacity.
- An appointment is not considered rescheduled until a new date and time slot have been explicitly confirmed.
