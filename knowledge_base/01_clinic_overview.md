## 5. Clinical Departments Available
Medicare Multi-Specialty Clinic operates 6 dedicated outpatient clinical departments:
1. **General Medicine:** Comprehensive primary care, viral fever, hypertension, diabetes, and routine adult health evaluations.
2. **Cardiology:** Clinical cardiology consultations, preventive heart checkups, and ECG reviews.
3. **Orthopedics:** Bone, joint, fracture review, arthritis management, and spine consultations.
4. **Dental:** Routine dental checks, cleanings, toothache treatments, and oral hygiene care.
5. **Pediatrics:** Child healthcare, newborn checkups, infant nutrition, and growth monitoring.
6. **Dermatology:** Skin conditions, acne, eczema, fungal infections, hair, and scalp consultations.

## 8. Important Limitations & Safety Boundaries
- **Fictional Entity:** This clinic profile is fictional demo data for testing an AI receptionist.
- **No Emergency Medical Care:** The clinic is an outpatient facility and does not have an Emergency Room (ER) or Intensive Care Unit (ICU).
- **Emergency Protocol:** For acute life-threatening medical emergencies (e.g., severe chest pain, sudden paralysis, unconsciousness, massive trauma), callers must immediately contact local emergency services (112 / 108) or visit a tertiary multi-specialty hospital with an emergency room.
- **No In-House Ambulance:** The clinic does not operate an ambulance fleet.
- **No Advanced Imaging:** The clinic does not have MRI or CT scan facilities on-site.
